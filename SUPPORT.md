# Getting support

## Choose the right channel

**Documentation first.** Most questions are answered at
https://www.art2link.com/documentation/, particularly the Known Issues and Troubleshooting
sections.

**Discussions → Q&A** for anything of the form *"how do I…"*, *"is it possible
to…"*, or *"why does it behave like…"*. Answers get marked and become part of
the searchable archive, which helps the next person with the same question.

**Issues** only for defects: something that does not behave as documented, and
that you can describe with reproduction steps.

**Private security reporting** for vulnerabilities. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

**Commercial support** at https://www.art2link.com/contact/ for licensing,
migration assistance, SLAs, and anything covered by a contract. Community
support here is best-effort and carries no response guarantee.

## Response expectations

This tracker is monitored on business days. Triage typically happens within two
business days. Community members answer Discussions on their own schedule.

If you have a support contract, use your contracted channel — filing here does
not start an SLA clock.

## Before you post

- Search existing issues and Discussions. Duplicates are common and get closed.
- Confirm you are on a supported version.
- Redact credentials, connection strings, internal hostnames, and customer data.
